# Adventure rivals (Ryan's notes, 10 October 2026)

Semi-rare encounters, mostly toward the higher levels unless noted. Each gets a character card in the Gallery
(sprite, name, your record against them) and speech bubbles at the start and end of a duel.

## Characters
- **Alert Horse** - builds a big opener (2+ swords, donkey+) through cunning. Beat him by sending a smaller opener first to interrupt.
- **Time Traveller** - 1.5x normal speed; about 1 game in 10 fills its own column 4 by accident when it gets full. Rages and calls you a modder (1 in 5 encounters). Can appear at any point in a run.
- **Demonic Toad** - obfuscates its board; strong; makes excuses when it loses (1 in 5 encounters).
- **Steve (is a turkey)** - only ever flips with the down arrow, never up. Insanely risky combos while quite defensive, and they somehow work. A bogey: pretty powerful.
- **Scuppered Turtle** - slow but methodical; large defensive clears over offensive ones.
- **Tina the Tuna** - just a decent player; can appear at any time.
- **Old-man JD** - airstrikes into the battle. Strong fighter, sore back, won back-to-back OSL titles, retired.
- **Confined Witch** - like Steve but riskier, more fun, more mistakes; less defensive, more offensive.
- **Sneaky Swapper** - changes their sword pattern once per game (a quick, unobtrusive alert and a chat line).
- **Crusty Innkeeper** - an early-game opponent; wields a stick; quick but makes fundamental errors.
- **Drunken Sailor** - drifts and fumbles, with the odd absurd lucky combo.
- **Clockwork Golem** - places a pair exactly every 1.2 s, faster each round; a race.
- **The Monk** - never attacks, only clears; the encounter is "send X swords before time runs out".
- **The Painter** - stacks one favourite colour into huge blocks, then dumps it all at once.
- **Dragon on its Hoard** - builds one huge combo without sending anything (a glow grows); your attacks landing on its board break up the setup and cut the hoard short.
- **Berserker** - faster and riskier the higher its stack.
- **Sleepy Blacksmith** - slow, sometimes nods off for a few seconds; wakes angry and fast if you hit it.
- **The Gambler** - flips a coin each pair between very safe and all-in.
